Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do we have to replace our software?

Usually not. Most of the value comes from connecting what you already run and own. Replacement is a last resort, not a starting point.

Do you resell licences?

No. You buy directly from the vendor, which keeps the pricing visible and our advice honest.

How long does a project take?

A single process is often a few weeks. We deliver in stages so something useful lands early rather than everything arriving at the end.

What if the person who built it leaves?

That's why documentation is part of every engagement. The system should be understandable by someone who wasn't there when it was built.

Can you guarantee it won't break?

No. Third-party software changes and things fail. We monitor, document and respond quickly, which is what can honestly be promised.

Do you work with our existing IT provider?

Yes, routinely. We handle systems and data; they handle devices and infrastructure, and we'll coordinate rather than compete.

Someone re-typing the same data?

A free review of your systems and a straight answer on what's worth automating first.
