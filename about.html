How we work

Map it, connect it, document it

Duplication is the root of most reporting problems. When a record exists in several places, each copy drifts, and reconciling them becomes a monthly job nobody enjoys or trusts.

Fixing it starts with watching the work rather than reading the process document, which is usually out of date and describes what someone intended rather than what happens.

Then it's one process at a time, documented as it's built. Big-bang projects fail because everything changes at once and nobody can tell which part broke.
